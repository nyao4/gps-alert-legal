3. Location and background operation

The App uses Android location permissions and location services such as Google Play services. If precise location is not authorized, accuracy and available functions may be limited.

For enabled alerts or automatic place recording/timelines enabled in settings, location may be collected while the App is not in the foreground or the screen is off, subject to settings, permissions, and OS controls. The monitoring service uses an ongoing device notification and may resume after a device restart according to settings.

Location is used to detect entry/exit, record stays and times, and show places on maps. Coordinates and search areas may be sent to external map/geocoding services to obtain addresses or facility names.

Turning off automatic place recording does not necessarily stop location monitoring for enabled alerts. To stop collection, disable the relevant monitoring/recording settings or revoke location permission in Android.

4. Google account integration

The App displays Google's account selection/authorization screens as needed. API operations target the account authorized in that process, rather than collecting data from every account signed into the device.

  • Google Calendar: the schedule page reads, creates, updates, and deletes events in the primary calendar. Diary entries saved to Google use the separate “GPS Alert Diary” calendar. Synchronization may also retrieve events created outside this App in the relevant calendars.
  • Google Tasks: reads, creates, updates, and deletes authorized task lists and tasks.
  • Google Drive: used for backups/restoration and storing/retrieving diary attachments according to settings.

The App requests OAuth scopes calendar, tasks, drive.appdata, and drive.file according to the functions used. Calendar permission, in particular, is not limited to the single event currently displayed.

Google API data is used only to provide user-facing event, diary, and task display, editing, synchronization, backups, and related functions. It is not used for advertising, data sales, credit decisions, or training general-purpose AI models. Use and transfer of Google API information to other apps follow the Limited Use requirements of the Google API Services User Data Policy.

The Operator does not ordinarily have people read Google API data. Any necessary human access for support is limited to the scope of your express consent or necessary access permitted for legal/security purposes and other allowed cases.

7. Permissions and choices

You can select location, background location, notification, and Google permissions through OS/Google authorization screens and App settings. Images are those you select through the OS picker. The reviewed implementation does not request access to the camera, microphone, or device contacts.

Refusing/revoking permission can prevent or limit the relevant functions. Revoking notification permission alone does not necessarily stop all location monitoring, recording, or cloud synchronization.

Displaying this Policy does not constitute blanket consent to every permission or external transmission. Necessary explanations and permissions are handled separately for the relevant functions.

8. Retention

  • Alerts, events, diaries, tasks, and places: retained on the device until you delete them or similar action occurs. Some history is subject to configured item limits and cleanup.
  • Timeline points and stay history: cleaned up according to retention settings. An unlimited setting disables time-based automatic deletion. Cleanup occurs when relevant processing runs; changing a setting does not necessarily erase everything immediately.
  • Pending synchronization: retained as necessary for retries until creation/update/deletion completes or similar processing ends. Offline operation, authorization issues, or quotas may delay completion.
  • Google data: retained until deletion in the relevant service or other service action. Reducing timeline retention does not automatically delete Google Calendar records, Drive images, or backups.
  • Backups and shared copies: may remain until deleted at each destination. Restoration may return previously deleted information to the device.
  • Support information: kept only as needed for support, disputes/fraud prevention, legally required retention, and related purposes, then deleted or otherwise appropriately handled.

9. Stopping, disconnecting, and deleting

On-device records and Google diary entries

Use the diary page's diary-data deletion menu to choose local and/or Google Calendar data and all records or a date range. Local diary deletion includes relevant on-device attachments covered by the operation, but is not the same as deleting all raw timeline location history.

If Google deletion is incomplete because of connectivity, quotas, or other limits, check the result. Retries may be needed; a local operation does not guarantee immediate cloud deletion.

Events, tasks, alerts, and other device data

Use the corresponding editing/deletion functions. To remove all on-device App data, clear storage in Android's App settings or uninstall the App. Delete files copied/shared elsewhere separately.

Drive images and backups

Deleting diary entries or uninstalling does not automatically delete attachments/backups in Google Drive. Delete images in ordinary Drive folders through Drive; check hidden app data through Google's app-management tools. Follow Google's guidance about trash and service-side retention.

Disconnecting Google

Revoke access through Google account connections. Revocation restricts future access; it does not delete data already stored on the device or events/files in Google. Complete necessary cloud deletion before revoking access, or delete directly through Google.

10. Security and precautions

The App uses Android's app storage/permission controls, Google's authorization mechanisms, and HTTPS for Google REST API communications. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled in the reviewed configuration. This does not guarantee the behavior of every device/OS migration or backup mechanism.

Local JSON backups are not encrypted by an App-specific encryption layer. Lock your device and store backups/exports in trusted locations. This storage design is not described as end-to-end encryption.

Images may retain original metadata. Notifications/popups can show place labels and notes, so review lock-screen notification settings. Shared-calendar/folder settings affect information stored there.

If unauthorized access or a leak is identified, the Operator will investigate and respond within its control and make legally required reports and notifications to affected individuals.

11. Advertising, analytics, and AI

The reviewed App contains no advertising SDK, Operator-run behavioral analytics infrastructure, or function sending events, diaries, or location history to external generative AI APIs. Automatic place recording uses location/stay decisions and address/facility information.

Google Maps/Places and other SDKs nevertheless process diagnostics, device information, and related data. The absence of an advertising SDK does not mean there is no external transmission or SDK data collection.

12. Minors

Minors should review location and Google integration with a parent/guardian and obtain consent where required. Respect other people's rights when entering/sharing their events, images, or location information.
